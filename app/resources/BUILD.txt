Print the four small fit coupons first: male and female dovetail, then male and female pin. Tune dovetail_clearance and pin_clearance for your printer before printing full modules. Clearances are radial, in millimeters. Standard-width modules use four top pins in a two-by-two pattern.
Print remaining STLs according to configuration.json quantities. Assemble columns left to right, then stack rows from bottom to top. Top rail sections follow the same column order. For a double-hung sash, install continuous compressible gasket tape along the channel bottom and both side walls. Measure the actual lower sash thickness; the sash must lower into the printed channel and compress the gasket without bottoming out on hard plastic. The hardware list gives a three-run tape length allowance. A rail height of 0 does not provide a sash seal.
Every wide solid or filler cell has a closing back skin. If foam is selected, cut rigid closed-cell foam to the foam_cuts dimensions and fit it into each cassette before fastening its back skin. Seal every back-skin perimeter seam. For each fan cover, insert two magnets into the bottom pockets from the shell side, push the printed magnet spacers behind them, and bond the shell to the back skin to trap the magnets mechanically. Fasten two sized countersunk steel strike discs into the fan housing bottom recesses with countersunk M3 screws. Apply compressed-thickness gasket tape to the cover perimeter, keeping the magnet contact pads clear. Lift the cover about 5 mm, insert the two top hooks into their keyhole openings, lower it, and let the bottom magnets catch. Pull the bottom edge to remove; remove covers before running fans.
Install each 120 mm fan from the exterior through the unobstructed square housing pocket, then secure its four standard mounting holes from the room side through the front grille. Four M3 screws fasten each output louver into separate pilot holes in the solid rear outer corners. Select screw lengths for the actual prints.
Seal room-side module joints and use compressible perimeter weatherstripping where the kit meets the window. Route any cable through a properly sealed gland; the controller lid is solid. Keep exterior water draining outward. Alignment studs are not retention clips. Secure the assembly in the window.
