04 / TOP RAIL

The lowered sash seats into the top rail’s continuous U channel. Apply closed-cell gasket tape along the bottom and both side walls. Channel width is calculated from the measured sash thickness and compressed side gaskets. A 0 mm rail cannot seal a double-hung sash. Fit sealing rail may reduce a row if the opening is too short for both rows and the rail.

05 / FAN OUTPUT

The fan slides in from outdoors through an unobstructed square pocket. Louvers screw into the solid rear outer corners with M3 screws; no tabs occupy that pocket. The 116 mm outlet still adds flow resistance. For maximum flow in a protected location, omit it.

06 / INSULATION

Each cover has two top hooks and two trapped bottom magnets. Print two magnet spacers per cover. Use countersunk steel strike discs and M3 screws in the recessed housing seats. The strike diameter is kept at least as large as the magnet. Bond the shell to its back skin after inserting magnets and spacers. Lift about 5 mm to engage the hooks, then let the magnets close the bottom.
